    What's this card worth?

    Type the card in, then check what it really sold for. Sold prices are the best guide: what people paid, not what sellers are asking.

    Getting an accurate price
    • Match the exact version. Foils, parallels, and numbered cards can sell for 10 to 25 times the regular card.
    • Look at several recent sales in the same condition, not the single highest one.
    • Graded cards (PSA, BGS, CGC) sell for very different prices than raw ones.
